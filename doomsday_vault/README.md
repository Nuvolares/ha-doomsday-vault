# Doomsday Vault

Your preparedness vault in the Home Assistant side panel: an offline survival
library, a supply inventory and an armory.

When the grid goes down, the internet goes with it. Doomsday Vault keeps a
copy of the knowledge you would want on your own disk, sorted by category and
searchable, and keeps it up to date automatically while you still have a
connection. Next to it, it tracks what you have stocked.

- Complete English Wikipedia with images
- Medical references: WikiMed, MedlinePlus, CDC, military medicine
- Preparedness: Ready.gov (FEMA), US Army publications, water, knots, post-disaster guides
- Repair and DIY: iFixit, home improvement, vehicles, electronics
- Food and farming, energy, ham radio, science textbooks, public-domain books
- Offline map of North America (USA, Canada, Mexico)
- One search box across everything
- Add your own sources (Kiwix archives or any PDF/file link)
- Scheduled updates that only download what changed
- Inventory of food, water, medical supplies and gear with expiration dates,
  "use or replace soon" warnings and photos
- Armory synced with WeaponLog (weapons, ammunition, accessories) that keeps
  working offline and sends your changes when WeaponLog is back

About 210 GB with the default selection. Large extras, such as Khan Academy and
all of Project Gutenberg, can be switched on.
